Guitar-TECHS dataset — player 1, direct-input channel
Pedroza, H. et al., "Guitar-TECHS: An Electric Guitar Dataset Covering
Techniques, Musical Excerpts, Chords and Scales Using a Diverse Array of
Hardware", ICASSP 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03720
Data: https://zenodo.org/records/14963133  ·  https://guitar-techs.github.io/

Single notes and palm mutes were cut from the long DI
recordings (one note per 4 s window), trimmed, RMS-normalised and encoded
to MP3 for RiffSmith by Matt Hartman, 2026. Files: n<midi>_s<string>.mp3
(sustain), pm<midi>_s<string>.mp3 (palm mute), ph<midi>_s<string>.mp3
string 1 = high E, 6 = low E.
Modification (2026-09-02): the low E string (n*_s6) on this recording had
almost no upper partials (its 1-2 kHz content sat ~28 dB below the same
pitches on the A string). Those files had their partials 4-12 regenerated
from the recorded fundamentals and fitted to the A-string spectrum. The
original cuts are the dataset's; the restored files are RiffSmith's.
