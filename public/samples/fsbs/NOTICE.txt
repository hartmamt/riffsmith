Direct (DI) recordings of a Fender electric guitar, bridge pickup, standard
tuning, two dynamics x round robins, 48 kHz. Trimmed to 3.5 s, level-matched
(hard x0.45, soft x0.20) and encoded to MP3 for RiffSmith by Matt Hartman, 2026.
Files: n<midi>_<h|s>_rr<n>.mp3 (h = hard pick, 4 RR; s = soft pick, 2 RR).
